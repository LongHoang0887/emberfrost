Emberfrost support

Emberfrost is a tower defense game for iPhone. It is in testing on TestFlight.

Contact

Write to longvnh06@gmail.com. If something went wrong, these help:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version
  • the level you were on and what you did just before
  • a screenshot, if you have one

Testers can also send feedback from the TestFlight app: open Emberfrost there and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Purchases

Apple handles every payment. If a purchase does not show up in the game, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. For a refund, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

The game has no account. Your progress is saved on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes it.
